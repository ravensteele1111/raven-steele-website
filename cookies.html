Good news

Cookies

This website does not use cookies to track you, which is why you have not been asked about any.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

This website does not use cookies to track you. There is no advertising, no analytics watching where you go, and no cookie banner, because there is nothing to ask you about.

What a cookie is

A small file a website leaves on your device so it can remember something. Most sites use them to follow people around the internet. This one does not.

The one thing we do store

If you unlock the extra photos by giving your email address, your own browser remembers that you did, so you are not asked again every time you come back. That note sits on your device and goes nowhere. Clearing your browser data removes it, and you would simply be asked once more.

Videos

The music plays through YouTube. Nothing is requested from YouTube until you actually press play — until then you are looking at a picture stored on this site. When you do press play, YouTube may set cookies of its own, under their rules. We use their no-cookie player, which keeps that to a minimum.

Where this site lives

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, who keep ordinary server records as every host does. Those are not cookies and we do not use them.

If that changes

If anything is ever added that does set tracking cookies, this page changes first and you will be asked properly.
