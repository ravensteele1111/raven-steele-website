Privacy

If you give us your email address, we email you. That is the whole of it.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Marketing Ambition PTY LTD, Australia. If you want to ask anything about your information, or want it deleted, write to aiinfluencerraven@gmail.com and we will sort it out.

What we collect

Your email address, and only if you hand it over. That happens when you join the Rebels, ask for the free gift, or put your name down for the merch waitlist. Nothing on this site collects anything about you until you type it in and press a button.

When you sign up we also record which of Watch, Listen or Learn you chose. That is so we send you things you actually care about rather than everything we have.

What we do with it

We email you: new songs, behind the scenes, photos that never make Instagram, and anything else worth sending. We do not sell your address, rent it, or hand it to anybody for their own use.

The list itself is kept with our email provider, who store it on our behalf and do not use it for anything of their own.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you are off the list, and we will not email you again. You can also write to us and ask us to delete your address entirely, which we will do.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If a child has signed up, write to us and we will remove it.

Other people’s websites

This site links out to YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, Instagram, Skool and the shop. Once you follow one of those links you are on their website, under their rules, not ours. The same goes for anything you buy: the shop is somebody else’s checkout, and your payment details never pass through this site.

Where this website lives

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web host they keep ordinary server records, which can include the address of the device asking for a page. We do not look at those records and nothing is tied back to you by us.

A word about Raven

Raven, Ethan and everyone else in this world are characters. The stories, the events and the photographs are created, not reported, and nobody in them is a real person you could meet.

Changes

If this ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
